FlowXport Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 27, 2026

1. Our Commitment

FlowXport is built on a "Zero-Knowledge" architecture. We respect the sensitivity of your Salesforce data and have designed this tool so that your data remains yours.

2. How We Handle Data

  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) Model: FlowXport operates on a "Bring Your Own Key" basis. When you input your OpenAI API key, you are directly authorizing the OpenAI service to process your data under your own account credentials.
  • No Middle-Man: Because you provide your own key, the data transmission happens directly between your browser and OpenAI’s servers. FlowXport does not facilitate, store, or act as an intermediary for this data transmission.
  • No Data Storage: FlowXport does not store, host, or log your Salesforce metadata on any servers. Your Flow data exists only in your browser's local memory during the documentation process.
  • Your API Key: Your OpenAI API key is stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local). It is never transmitted to us or any third party.

3. Payments and Subscriptions

FlowXport uses Lemon Squeezy as our third-party payment processor to manage subscriptions and license access. We do not store, process, or have access to your full credit card information, bank details, or sensitive financial data. All payment transactions are handled securely by Lemon Squeezy in accordance with their own Privacy Policy.

4. OpenAI Compliance

FlowXport uses the OpenAI API. We ensure that our configuration does not allow OpenAI to use your submitted data for model training.

5. Data Control

As the owner of the API key, you maintain full control over your data usage and costs through the OpenAI platform.

6. Changes to Policy

Any updates to this policy will be reflected here. By using FlowXport, you acknowledge that you are using your own API credentials and that you are responsible for the data you process.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or the way FlowXport handles your data, please feel free to reach out to our support team:
