FlowXport

SALESFORCE FLOW DOCUMENTATION

FlowXport is a lightweight Chrome extension built for Salesforce Admins and Developers who are tired of manually documenting complex automations or trying to reverse-engineer canvas spaghetti left behind years ago.

  • Automated Documentation: Instantly generate clean, readable documentation straight from your Salesforce Flow Builder.
  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key): Keeps your data private and secure under your own API credentials with zero third-party storage hassles.
  • Built for Daily Use: Designed to slot seamlessly into your normal development and administration workflow.

FlowXport operates on a BYOK model to keep your data secure under your own credentials, giving you seamless documentation right inside your normal admin and development workflow.
